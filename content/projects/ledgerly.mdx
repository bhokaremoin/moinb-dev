## The problem

My flat tracked shared costs in a Google Sheet. Nobody updated it, and every month ended with an awkward conversation about who owed what.

## Constraints

- Must work on cheap Android phones over slow data.
- No app install. Flatmates would not download another app.
- Zero hosting budget beyond free tiers.

## Key decisions

**tRPC over REST.** One developer, one codebase. End-to-end types removed a whole class of bugs and I didn't need a public API.

**Store balances as a ledger, not totals.** Every expense is an immutable row, and balances are computed. This made "undo" and history trivial.
