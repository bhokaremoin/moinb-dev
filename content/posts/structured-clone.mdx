`structuredClone` deep-copies objects, including `Date`, `Map` and `Set`, without `JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(...))` tricks.

```js title="clone.js"
const copy = structuredClone({ when: new Date(), tags: new Set(["a"]) });
```
